\begin{abstract}
We present in full both (1)~the complete original geometric and physical framework of the \textbf{Frohmanian Symplectic Tether} $\Tf$ (preserving without omission all original definitions, physical motivations, conjectured geometric--analytic bridges, and extensions from \emph{Frohmanian\_Tether\_Final\_Submission\_v8}), and (2)~its complete, kernel-verified, non-vacuous conditional formalization in Lean~4 (repository \texttt{frohmanian-tether-sdiff}: \textbf{519 theorems in the \texttt{FrohmanianTether.*} modules, zero \texttt{sorryAx}, and only the standard axioms \texttt{propext}, \texttt{Classical.choice}, \texttt{Quot.sound}}, as counted by \texttt{scripts/AxiomAudit.lean}). This manuscript is \textbf{not peer-reviewed} and is \textbf{not} an unconditional Navier--Stokes proof.

Let $M(t)=\|\omega(t)\|_{L^\infty}$ denote the vorticity sup-norm (\lean{vorticity_sup_norm}) along a physical solution (\lean{IsPhysicalNSSolution}) of the three-dimensional incompressible Navier--Stokes equations with smooth, divergence-free, finite-energy initial data and viscosity $\nu>0$. We prove in Lean~4 (\lean{frohmanian_tether_theorem} and \lean{conditional_regularity_of_open_hyps}) that if (i)~Kato local existence holds with a uniform restart length on the class of physical solutions (\lean{IsPhysicalNSSolution}, enforcing pointwise time-differentiability and the Leray $L^2$ energy inequality), (ii)~uniqueness holds within \lean{IsPhysicalNSSolution}, and (iii)~$M(t)$ satisfies the Frohmanian cubic differential inequality (\lean{FrohmanianTetherRate})
\[
M'(t)\le \CZ\,M(t)^2-\kappa''\,M(t)^3,\qquad t\ge 0,
\]
for constants $\CZ,\kappa''>0$, then a scalar comparison argument (\lean{comparison_ode_stability}) yields the uniform ceiling $M(t)\le\max\bigl(M(0),\CZ/\kappa''\bigr)$ (\lean{FrohmanianTetherBound}), and, as a Lean-checked implication from these open hypotheses, the Beale--Kato--Majda criterion together with uniform Kato restart yields a smooth, divergence-free physical solution.

Crucially, the time-smoothness and physical-energy repair (\lean{IsPhysicalNSSolution}) is \textbf{fully implemented and kernel-verified non-vacuous}: in \lean{ClayStatements.lean}, we prove that unphysical time-step fields are excluded (\lean{stepField_not_physical}) and that all hypotheses of \lean{conditional_regularity_of_open_hyps} (\lean{repaired_hInt}, \lean{repaired_huniq_at_zero}, \lean{frohmanianTetherBound_zero}, \lean{frohmanianTetherRate_zero}) hold simultaneously and unconditionally at $u_0=0$ (\lean{conditional_regularity_nonvacuous_at_zero}). Furthermore, every original conjectured bridge—including \lean{reduced_pairing_of_unmodified_and_tether} (via \lean{ConjecturedUnmodifiedTetherPairing}), \lean{tethered_stretching_of_weight}, \lean{tether_coadjoint_invariance_of_integral_eq} (via \lean{ConjecturedSDiffPushforwardInvariance}), and the symmetric uniqueness characterization (\lean{uniqueness_of_minimal_tether}, which delegates to \lean{uniqueness_of_minimal_symmetric_tether})—is compiled in the Lean build with zero \lean{sorry}s.
\paragraph{Status of the damping premise.} The full tethered bracket $\{F,G\}_{\Tf}=\{F,G\}_{\mathrm{cl}}+T(F,G)$ is of \emph{metriplectic type}: an antisymmetric Lie--Poisson part plus a symmetric (\lean{tetherKernel_symmetric}), negative-semidefinite (\lean{tetherKernel_diagonal_nonpos}) tether $T$, with the classical part antisymmetric (\lean{classicalBracket_antisymm}). The Jacobi identity of the classical part is cited from \cite{Arnold1966}, not formalized. It is \emph{not} antisymmetric and no Jacobi identity is claimed for it. Because $T$ is symmetric and degenerate on the kinetic energy (C2), $T(M,H)=0$ for every functional $M$ (\lean{tetherKernel_left_H_zero}), so the bracket contributes \emph{no} damping to the evolution of any functional, including $M(t)=\|\omega\|_{L^\infty}$: the damping coefficient derived from the bracket is $0$. The cubic rate (R) and the resulting closing bound are therefore \textbf{open assumptions}, not consequences of the tether. Every statement below that passes through (R)---the Riccati/cubic comparison ceiling and the conditional regularity theorems---depends on this unresolved premise. \textbf{No regularity claim for the Navier--Stokes equations is made.}

\section{Introduction}\label{sec:intro}

The incompressible Navier--Stokes equations on $\T=\R^3/\mathbb{Z}^3$ (or on $\R^3$ with finite energy) are (Lean: \lean{NS_PDE})
\begin{equation}\label{eq:NS}
\partial_t u+(u\cdot\nabla)u+\nabla p=\nu\Delta u,\qquad \nabla\cdot u=0,\qquad u(0)=u_0,\qquad\text{(\lean{NS_PDE})}
\end{equation}
with $\nu>0$. Taking the curl yields (\lean{vorticity_transport_at}, under the regularity hypothesis \lean{VorticityTransportRegularity}) the vorticity transport equation for $\omega=\nabla\times u$:
\begin{equation}\label{eq:vort}
\partial_t\omega+(u\cdot\nabla)\omega=(\omega\cdot\nabla)u+\nu\Delta\omega .
\end{equation}
By the Beale--Kato--Majda theorem \cite{BKM1984} (cited, not formalized), a smooth solution can be continued past $T$ whenever $\int_0^T\|\omega(t)\|_{L^\infty}\,dt<\infty$. A uniform ceiling on $M(t)=\|\omega(t)\|_{L^\infty}$ therefore implies, by BKM (cited, not formalized), smooth continuation of that solution. Obtaining such a bound for arbitrary large smooth data is the core difficulty of the Clay Millennium Problem \cite{Fefferman2006} (background, not formalized): the vortex-stretching term $(\omega\cdot\nabla)u$ is supercritical with respect to the natural parabolic scaling
\begin{equation}\label{eq:scaling}
u_\lambda(x,t)=\lambda u(\lambda x,\lambda^2t),\qquad \omega_\lambda(x,t)=\lambda^2\omega(\lambda x,\lambda^2 t),\qquad\text{(at each fixed time: \lean{ScalingIdentities.vorticity_rescale})}
\end{equation}
which leaves \eqref{eq:NS} invariant at fixed $\nu$.

The Frohmanian programme approaches this problem through a two-layer architecture:
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Geometric layer (\texttt{ArnoldGeometric.lean}, \texttt{SymplecticTether.lean}, \texttt{Uniqueness.lean}, \texttt{ForMathlib/Projection.lean}):} Starting from Arnold's coadjoint orbit of volume-preserving diffeomorphisms \cite{Arnold1966,ArnoldKhesin1998}, we introduce the \textbf{Frohmanian Symplectic Tether} $\Tf$—realized concretely via the $L^2$-projected quadratic kernel \lean{TetherKernel} with universal Calderón--Zygmund coupling $\kappa=\CZ(3)=3/2$. By projecting functional derivatives orthogonally to the velocity field $u=\delta H/\delta\omega$ via the Gram--Schmidt projector $\Pi_u$ (\lean{Pi_u}), the tether satisfies exact degeneracy $B(F,H)=0$ on the kinetic-energy Hamiltonian $H$ (\lean{tetherKernel_degenerates_on_kinetic_energy}), leaving the reversible Euler vector field unmodified while supplying a negative-semidefinite diagonal value $T(F,F)=-\kappa\|\omega\|_{L^2}^2\int|\Pi_u\delta F/\delta\omega|^2$ (\lean{ProducesControllableNegativeFeedback}). The same degeneracy implies that the tether induces no damping of $M(t)$ (Section~\ref{sec:damping}; \lean{tetherKernel_left_H_zero}).
\item \textbf{Analytic layer (\texttt{NS\_Equations.lean}, \texttt{TetheredLyapunov.lean}, \texttt{IndependentMajorant.lean}, \texttt{CubicSourceCertificate.lean}, \texttt{AnalyticPipeline.lean}, \texttt{ClosureBlueprint.lean}, \texttt{ConditionalRegularity.lean}, \texttt{ClayStatements.lean}):} Using the quartic Lyapunov functional $S_\varepsilon(t)$ weighted by the transported enstrophy-accumulation scalar $\varphi$ (\lean{LyapunovS}, \lean{IsEnstrophyAccumulation}), the programme links the cubic rate $M'(t)\le \CZ M(t)^2-\kappa'' M(t)^3$ (\lean{FrohmanianTetherRate})---an \emph{open assumption} not derived from the tether (Section~\ref{sec:damping})---to the autonomous comparison ODE $y'=Cy^2-\kappa''y^3$ (\lean{comparison_ode_stability}, \lean{comparison_majorant_global_bound}), yielding the uniform ceiling $M(t)\le\max(M(0),\CZ/\kappa'')$ (\lean{riccati_ceiling_of_vorticity_di}) and, \emph{conditionally on that assumption and the further open hypotheses (K), (U)}, the smooth-continuation conclusion of Theorem~\ref{thm:main}.
\end{enumerate}

\begin{theorem}[Conditional regularity implication from open hypotheses; \lean{frohmanian_tether_theorem}, \lean{conditional_regularity_of_open_hyps}; depends on the unresolved premise (R)]\label{thm:main}
Let $\nu>0$ and let $u_0$ be smooth (\lean{ContDiff ℝ ⊤}), divergence-free, and of finite $L^2$ energy. Assume within the class of physical solutions (\lean{IsPhysicalNSSolution}):
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(K)] \emph{Kato local existence with uniform restart}: a local smooth physical solution (\lean{KatoLocalWitness}) exists from every such datum, with a restart increment $\tau>0$ extending existence times below $T^*(u_0,\nu)$;
\item[(R)] \emph{Frohmanian cubic tether rate (\lean{FrohmanianTetherRate})}: along every physical solution from $u_0$, the vorticity sup-norm $t\mapsto M(t)$ is continuous, differentiable on $[0,\infty)$, and satisfies
\[
M'(t)\le \CZ\,M(t)^2-\kappa''\,M(t)^3\qquad(t\ge 0)
\]
for constants $\CZ>0$, $\kappa''>0$ (yielding \lean{FrohmanianTetherBound});
\item[(U)] \emph{Physical uniqueness (\texttt{huniq})}: smooth physical solutions (\lean{IsPhysicalNSSolution}) starting from $u_0$ agree with the Kato witness on each existence interval $[0,T]$.
\end{enumerate}
Then $M(t)\le\max\bigl(M(0),\CZ/\kappa''\bigr)$ for all $t\ge0$, and \eqref{eq:NS} admits a physical solution defined $u$ on $[0,\infty)$ that is spatially $C^\infty$ for every $t\ge0$, divergence-free, and unique among all smooth physical solutions.
\end{theorem}

\section{Setting and the Physical Solution Class}\label{sec:setting}

Write $M(t)=\|\omega(t)\|_{L^\infty}$ (Lean: \lean{vorticity_sup_norm (vorticity (u t))}). In the Lean formalization, the pointwise Navier--Stokes equation is \lean{NS_PDE u p ν}. Because Mathlib's \lean{deriv} returns $0$ at points where a function of time is not differentiable, a spatially smooth field with jump discontinuities in time would trivially satisfy \lean{NS_PDE} unless time-differentiability and energy control are enforced.

We therefore define the physical solution class \lean{IsPhysicalNSSolution} in \lean{NS_Equations.lean}:
\leanfile{IsPhysicalNSSolution}
A time-dependent velocity field $u$ belongs to \lean{IsPhysicalNSSolution} if and only if for all $t\ge0$:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $s\mapsto u(s,x)$ is pointwise differentiable at $t$ for every $x\in\R^3$ (field of \lean{IsPhysicalNSSolution});
\item $x\mapsto u(t,x)$ is continuous and has finite kinetic energy $\int_{\R^3}\|u(t,x)\|^2\,dx<\infty$ (field of \lean{IsPhysicalNSSolution});
\item $u$ obeys the classical Leray $L^2$ energy inequality $\int_{\R^3}\|u(t,x)\|^2\,dx\le\int_{\R^3}\|u(0,x)\|^2\,dx$ (field of \lean{IsPhysicalNSSolution});
\item $u(t)=u(0)$ for $t\le0$ (initial-value convention on $(-\infty,0]$; field of \lean{IsPhysicalNSSolution}).
\end{enumerate}

The classical PDE inputs enter as explicit, transparent hypotheses:
\begin{itemize}
\item Kato local existence on physical solutions (\lean{KatoLocalWitness}, carrying \lean{phys : IsPhysicalNSSolution u}) \cite{Kato1984,Temam1995};
\item Beale--Kato--Majda continuation and parabolic smoothing (\lean{bkm_regularity_pipeline}) \cite{BKM1984,ConstantinFoias1988};
\item Energy-Grönwall uniqueness of smooth physical solutions (\lean{uniqueness_of_existence_times_of_energy_gronwall}) \cite{Temam1995}.
\end{itemize}

\section{Geometric Layer: The Frohmanian Symplectic Tether}\label{sec:geom}

\subsection{The Classical Arnold Lie--Poisson Bracket}
On the coadjoint orbit $\mathcal O$ of the group $\mathrm{SDiff}(\T)$ of volume-preserving diffeomorphisms, the classical Kirillov--Kostant--Souriau / Arnold Lie--Poisson bracket \cite{Arnold1966,ArnoldKhesin1998} (Lean: \lean{ClassicalBracket}) is
\[
\{F,G\}_{\mathrm{cl}}(\omega)=\int_{\T}\omega\cdot\Bigl(\frac{\delta F}{\delta\omega}\times\frac{\delta G}{\delta\omega}\Bigr)\,dV .
\]
With kinetic-energy Hamiltonian $H(\omega)=\tfrac12\int|u|^2\,dV$ (where $\delta H/\delta\omega=u=\mathrm{BS}(\omega)$ is the Biot--Savart velocity reconstruction, formalized in \lean{ArnoldGeometric.lean}), the Hamiltonian vector field of $\{F,H\}_{\mathrm{cl}}$ reproduces the ideal 3D Euler vorticity equation ($\nu=0$ in \eqref{eq:vort}; Lean: \lean{classical_bracket_reproduces_Euler}).

\subsection{Route A: The Projected Tether Kernel and Degeneracy}
\begin{definition}[Frohmanian tether kernel; Lean \lean{TetherKernel}, \lean{Pi_u}]\label{def:tether}
For a velocity field $u=\mathrm{BS}(\omega)$ with $\|u\|_{L^2}\neq0$, let $\Pi_u=P^\perp_u$ be the $L^2$-orthogonal Gram--Schmidt projection onto the complement of $\operatorname{span}\{u\}$:
\[
\Pi_u v=v-\frac{\langle v,u\rangle_{L^2}}{\|u\|_{L^2}^2}\,u
\]
(Lean: \lean{ForMathlib.Pi_u}, with $\Pi_u v=v$ when $\|u\|_{L^2}=0$). With universal 3D Calderón--Zygmund constant $\kappa=\CZ(3)=\frac{3}{8\pi}\cdot 4\pi=\frac{3}{2}$ (Lean: \lean{kappa_eq_three_halves} in \lean{SymplecticTether.lean}), define the Frohmanian tether kernel
\[
T(F,G)(\omega)=B(F,G)(\omega)=-\kappa\,\|\omega\|_{L^2}^2\int_{\T}\Bigl\langle \Pi_u\Bigl(\frac{\delta F}{\delta\omega}\Bigr)(x),\,\Pi_u\Bigl(\frac{\delta G}{\delta\omega}\Bigr)(x)\Bigr\rangle\,dV ,
\]
where $\|\omega\|_{L^2}^2=\int_{\T}|\omega|^2\,dV$ is the enstrophy (Lean: \lean{Enstrophy}), a constant on each coadjoint orbit. The full tethered bracket is $\{F,G\}_{\Tf}=\{F,G\}_{\mathrm{cl}}+T(F,G)$.
\end{definition}

\begin{proposition}[Strict Degeneracy on Kinetic Energy; proved, \lean{tetherKernel_degenerates_on_kinetic_energy}]\label{prop:degen}
Whenever $\delta H/\delta\omega=u$, $\nabla\cdot u=0$, and $u\in L^2$, one has $\Pi_u u=0$ (\lean{projection_orthogonal_to_u}) and hence
\[
B(F,H)(\omega)=0\qquad\text{for every functional }F.
\]
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
If $\|u\|_{L^2}\ne0$, $\Pi_u u=u-(\langle u,u\rangle_{L^2}/\|u\|_{L^2}^2)u=0$, so the projected factor $\Pi_u(\delta H/\delta\omega)$ vanishes pointwise (\lean{projection_orthogonal_to_u}). If $\|u\|_{L^2}=0$, $u=0$ a.e.\ and $\Pi_u 0=0$ (\lean{Pi_u_zero}). Thus $\{F,H\}_{\Tf}=\{F,H\}_{\mathrm{cl}}$ for all $F$: the reversible Euler vector field generated by $H$ is preserved unmodified (\lean{tetherKernel_degenerates_on_kinetic_energy}).
\end{proof}

\subsection{Metriplectic Structure of the Tethered Bracket}\label{sec:metriplectic}
The tether $T$ of Definition~\ref{def:tether} is a \emph{symmetric} bilinear form in $(F,G)$ (\lean{tetherKernel_comm}, \lean{tetherKernel_symmetric}) and is negative-semidefinite, $T(F,F)=-\kappa\|\omega\|_{L^2}^2\int|\Pi_u\delta F/\delta\omega|^2\le0$ (\lean{ProducesControllableNegativeFeedback}). Hence
\[
\{F,G\}_{\Tf}=\underbrace{\{F,G\}_{\mathrm{cl}}}_{\text{antisymmetric, Lie--Poisson}}+\underbrace{T(F,G)}_{\text{symmetric, }\le0}\qquad\text{(\lean{tetherKernel_symmetric}, \lean{tetherKernel_diagonal_nonpos})}
\]
is a bracket of \emph{metriplectic type} in the sense of Morrison \cite{Morrison1986} (symmetry and sign: \lean{tetherKernel_symmetric}, \lean{tetherKernel_diagonal_nonpos}; antisymmetry of the classical part: \lean{classicalBracket_antisymm}). The full bracket is \textbf{not} antisymmetric, and \textbf{no Jacobi identity is claimed for it}; only its antisymmetric part $\{\cdot,\cdot\}_{\mathrm{cl}}$ is antisymmetric (\lean{classicalBracket_antisymm}). Its Jacobi identity is cited from \cite{Arnold1966}, not formalized. The symmetric part is nonzero off $H$ (\lean{tetherKernel_strict_neg_of_orthogonal_component}).

\paragraph{Energy conservation via (C2).} Since $\{H,H\}_{\mathrm{cl}}=0$ by antisymmetry (\lean{classicalBracket_H_H}) and $T(H,H)=0$ by kinetic-energy degeneracy (Proposition~\ref{prop:degen}, condition (C2), \lean{tetherKernel_degenerates_on_kinetic_energy}), the tethered flow $\dot F=\{F,H\}_{\Tf}$ conserves $H$: $\dot H=\{H,H\}_{\mathrm{cl}}+T(H,H)=0$. Here $T(H,H)=0$ is \lean{tetherKernel_degenerates_on_kinetic_energy}; $\{H,H\}_{\mathrm{cl}}=0$ is \lean{classicalBracket_H_H}.

\subsection{Canonicity: Symmetric Uniqueness}
In \lean{Uniqueness.lean}, the canonicity theorem \lean{uniqueness_of_minimal_tether} is stated for \emph{symmetric} kernels $B'(\omega,F,G)=B'(\omega,G,F)$ and delegates to \lean{uniqueness_of_minimal_symmetric_tether}: among symmetric bilinear kernels carrying the coadjoint-invariance (C1), kinetic-energy degeneracy (C2) and negative-feedback (C3) hypotheses, together with quadratic saturation (\lean{SaturatesTetherQuadratic}) and polarization of $B'$ and of \lean{TetherKernel} (\lean{Polarizes}), $B'$ equals \lean{TetherKernel}. The proof goes through polarization and saturation; the hypotheses (C1)--(C3) appear in the statement but are not used by the proof. The five-step route (\lean{uniqueness_of_minimal_tether_core}) uses the density hypothesis, the enstrophy weight $\alpha(\omega,x)=-c\,\|\omega\|_{L^2}^2$, saturation and a non-degeneracy witness. \lean{TetherKernel} itself is symmetric (\lean{tetherKernel_symmetric}).

\subsection{Coadjoint Invariance and the Conditional Jacobiator Lemma}
In \lean{SymplecticTether.lean}:
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Abstract \& push-forward coadjoint invariance:} \lean{tether_coadjoint_invariance} and \lean{tetherKernel_C1} verify \lean{InvariantUnderCoadjointAction TetherKernel} \emph{conditionally} on \lean{SDiffActionHyp} and \lean{TetherIntegralPreserved}. Here $\mathrm{SDiff}$ is an \emph{abstract} group acting on the orbit type (a Lean \texttt{MulAction}); it is not constructed as the diffeomorphism group of $\T$, and volume preservation is a \emph{hypothesis} on the field \texttt{diffeo} of \lean{VolumePreservingCoadjointAction}, not a theorem. A concrete instance is given in \texttt{ConcreteSDiff.lean}: \lean{ConcreteSDiff.SDiffConcrete} is a subgroup of \texttt{Equiv.Perm T3} whose elements $g$ are measurable with measurable inverse, \texttt{MeasurePreserving} for \texttt{volume} in both directions, and preserve divergence-freeness under composition (this last property is part of the definition, not a theorem). It (\lean{ConcreteSDiff.SDiffConcrete}) acts by scalar composition $g\cdot\omega=\omega\circ g^{-1}$, which is not the geometric push-forward $(Dg\,\omega)\circ g^{-1}$; no smoothness is imposed, and \texttt{T3} is \texttt{EuclideanSpace ℝ (Fin 3)}, not the quotient torus. For this action the field \texttt{casimir\_invariant} of \lean{SDiffActionHyp} is proved (\lean{ConcreteSDiff.casimir_invariant}, via \texttt{MeasurePreserving.integral\_comp'}), and \texttt{transitive\_on\_casimir\_levels} remains an open hypothesis (\lean{ConcreteSDiff.sdiffActionHyp_of_transitive}). \item \textbf{Calderón--Zygmund $L^\infty$ logarithm:} Singular integral operators (such as $\nabla u=\nabla\mathrm{BS}(\omega)$) are not bounded from $L^\infty$ to $L^\infty$ without a logarithmic Sobolev correction $\|\nabla u\|_{L^\infty}\lesssim \|\omega\|_{L^\infty}(1+\log^+(\|\omega\|_{H^s}/\|\omega\|_{L^\infty}))$ \cite{BKM1984} (cited, not formalized in the Lean library). In the pointwise local-maximum lemma, $\|\nabla u(x)\|\le\CZ\|\omega\|_{L^\infty}$ enters as the explicit hypothesis \lean{hCZ} of \lean{stretching_inner_le_at_max}.
\end{enumerate}
